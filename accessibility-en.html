Accessibility statement

Updated: September 14, 2026

Commitment and scope

TASHNAZ works to make the Kumu. website convenient and accessible. This statement covers the landing, information and support pages in Hebrew and English. It does not constitute an accessibility assessment of the iPhone app or Apple services.

Implemented features

Pages declare their language and direction and use structured headings, navigation landmarks, a main content area and a skip link. Links and expandable questions support keyboard operation and visible focus. App screenshots have descriptive text alternatives. Pages adapt to narrow screens and browser enlargement and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Keyboard and zoom

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between links, and Enter to activate them. Use Enter or Space on a question to expand or collapse its answer. The first link skips to main content. Use your browser zoom controls to enlarge the page; no accessibility plugin needs to be enabled.

Assessment and current status

The implementation aims to address applicable AA requirements of Israeli Standard SI 5568 Part 1. Development checks covered page structure, image descriptions, links, contrast, keyboard navigation and narrow layouts. No external certification or full screen-reader assessment has been completed. This statement is not a claim of complete conformance.

Report a barrier or request an alternative

Contact TASHNAZ at support@kumu.fun. Please include the page address, the problem, your device and browser, and assistive technology if relevant. No medical information is needed. You can request the website information in an accessible text format using the same channel. We will review your request and provide an update.

External services

Email links open your email application. Third-party interfaces may differ; contact us for assistance if you have trouble accessing information about Kumu.

The website code uses no cookies or tracking tools. Read our privacy policy for information about connection data and email enquiries. Cookies & privacy
